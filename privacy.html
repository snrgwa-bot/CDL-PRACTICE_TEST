Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Overview

CDL Practice Test ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Point

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your study progress is stored locally on your device only. The app sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server so we can improve it; you can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy (see section 4).

1. Information We Collect

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Browsing history or web activity
  • Financial or payment information (handled entirely by Apple)

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device using Apple's standard storage mechanisms (UserDefaults and local files) and is never transmitted to any server (your answers and scores are never included in the anonymous usage statistics described in section 4):

  • Quiz progress and scores
  • Study streak data
  • Spaced repetition review schedules
  • App preferences (theme, sound settings)
  • Subscription status

2. In-App Purchases

All purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID. Subscription management and billing are handled by Apple in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

3. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Its only usage statistics are our own, anonymous, first-party system described in section 4. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase
  • Facebook SDK or any social media trackers
  • Advertising networks or ad identifiers
  • Crash reporting services that collect personal data

4. Anonymous Usage Statistics

To learn which features help people and to find crashes, CDL Practice Test sends anonymous usage statistics to our own server (hosted on Supabase). This is our own first-party system — no third-party analytics or advertising SDK is involved. The app identifies itself only with a random installation ID that is created on your device and is deleted together with the app.

What is sent:

  • Device model, iOS version and app version
  • Language, region and time zone
  • Accessibility settings (such as larger text or reduced motion)
  • Session start and end, and the last screen you were on
  • App events, such as “a practice test was started” or “a practice test was completed”, with the test mode, number of questions, number of categories and a score range — never the questions or your answers
  • Paywall views and which plan was tapped
  • Purchase, trial, renewal-status and restore events (the product and plan length only)
  • Review-prompt requests
  • Crash and performance reports from Apple’s MetricKit

What is never sent: your name, email, phone number or address; your location; the advertising identifier (IDFA) or vendor identifier (IDFV); prices, receipts or transaction IDs; and anything you type, choose or create — including your answers. Your IP address is not stored or used.

Retention: usage statistics are kept for 13 months (crash and performance diagnostics for 6 months), then deleted.

Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When off, nothing is collected or sent, and anything not yet sent is deleted from your device.

Delete it: copy your Installation ID from Settings → Privacy and email it to essentialdevteam@gmail.com; we will delete everything linked to it.

5. Data Security

Since your study data is stored locally on your device, it benefits from Apple's built-in device security features including:

  • Device encryption (when passcode is enabled)
  • App sandboxing (data is isolated from other apps)
  • Secure Enclave protection on supported devices

If you delete the app, all locally stored data will be permanently removed from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed as an educational tool for individuals preparing for their Commercial Driver's License, which requires users to be at least 18 years old (or 21 for interstate driving).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Your Rights

We do not collect any personal data. Your locally stored app data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app. To have the anonymous usage statistics linked to your installation deleted from our server, email us your Installation ID (Settings → Privacy), or turn off Share Usage Data to stop sending them.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
